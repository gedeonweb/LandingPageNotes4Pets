2. Description of Service

Notes4Pets is a mobile application designed to help pet owners manage their pets' health and daily life. The App provides the following features:

  • Pet profile management (name, species, breed, birth date, personality)
  • Health record tracking (vaccines, treatments, veterinary visits, weight history)
  • Life journal with photos and mood tracking
  • Smart reminders and push notifications

The App is available in French, English, Spanish, and German, and automatically adapts to the device language.
